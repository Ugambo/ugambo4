# Phase 10 research notes: attractions, festivals and travel facts

This file records what we found about each place, where we found it, how confident we are, and where sources disagree. Use it to check the site's guide content before launch.

**Rule used for the site text:** we state only what at least one reasonable source supports. Where sources disagree, the site either says so ("sources differ") or leaves the detail out. Opening hours and entry fees were **not confirmed for any place**, so the site says "Not yet confirmed. Please check locally" rather than guess.

Every attraction imported into the site has **"Facts not yet verified on the ground"** ticked. Untick it in WP Admin → Attractions only after someone has checked the details locally, for example by visiting, calling the emirate or museum office, or asking the State tourism board.

Confidence levels:
- **High:** several independent sources agree.
- **Medium:** one good source, or several weaker ones that agree.
- **Low:** a single tourism blog or oral tradition. Shown on the site only as tradition ("tradition says…").

|---|---|---|

## 2. Emir of Katsina's Palace (Gidan Korau), Katsina city

| Fact | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Seat of the Emir of Katsina, in the centre of the old city | High | Wikipedia (Katsina, Kingdom of Katsina); Nigeria Galleria |
| Main gate is Kofar Soro (OSM pin 12.99467, 7.59928) | High | OpenStreetMap; Daily Trust (durbar route) |

## 3. Katsina ancient city walls and gates

| Fact | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The old city was surrounded by earth walls (ganuwa) about 21 km long (one source says 24 km) | Medium | Wikipedia (Katsina); Trust Radio "Nigeria's collapsing city walls" |
| Much of the wall has eroded; only parts survive | High | Trust Radio |
| Gate names given by sources: Kofar Guga, Sauri, Marusa, Kaura (Kwaura), Kwaya, Durbi, Keke, Yandaka | Medium (several tagged "citation needed" on Wikipedia) | Wikipedia "Gates of Hausa kingdoms" |
| Gate monuments mapped on OSM: Kofar Sauri 13.00955, 7.61389; Kofar Guga 12.99617, 7.58788; Kofar Durbi 12.99555, 7.61561; Kofar Kaura 12.97811, 7.60917 | High (location) | OpenStreetMap |

**Conflict:** a Sunday Trust/Trust Radio piece says Kofar Marusa (renovated 2007) is the only gate in its original form. Others say Kofar Sauri and Kofar Yandaka were restored. The site does not say which gate is "original".

**Founding of the city:** commonly given as about 1100 AD. This is traditional and approximate, so the site says "traditionally dated to around the 12th century".

Sources:

## 6. Zobe Dam (Dutsin-Ma LGA)

| Fact | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Dam on the Karaduwa River near Dutsin-Ma | High | Wikipedia "Zobe Dam" |
| Map pin 12.3883, 7.4747 | High | Wikipedia coordinates |
| Built 1972–1983 | Medium | Wikipedia |
| Storage about 179 million m³ (ThisDay 2017 says 140 million m³) | Medium (conflict) | Wikipedia; ThisDay |
| Long under-used. A water supply scheme was commissioned around 2020, and later expansion was announced | Medium | Daily Trust; ThisDay |

**Conflicts:** Wikipedia gives two different heights (48 m and 19 m), so the site gives no height. Storage figures differ, so the site gives both and says sources differ. The claim "largest dam in the state" is unverified and not used.

**Visiting:** dams are infrastructure. Public access may be restricted. The site says "ask locally before visiting".
