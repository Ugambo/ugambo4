Two brand directions for Explore Katsina

Both directions come from Katsina's own visual world rather than the usual travel-site blue and white. Each board shows the colours, the fonts, the map pins, the badges and a piece of the business dashboard, so you can see how the whole site would feel. Pick one, or mix them (for example A's colours with B's fonts).

Indigo & Laterite

Calm, crafted, heritage. Deep indigo from the dye pits, laterite red from the earth of the old city walls, ochre from harmattan light and savanna green. Headings use an elegant serif that feels like a museum label; body text is a clear, friendly sans-serif that reads well on small phones.

How to choose

  • A, Indigo & Laterite suits a heritage and tourism-first feel: trustworthy, calm, close to the city walls and dye pits.
  • B, Durbar Regalia suits a lively, events-and-booking feel: festive, eye-catching, more like a festival poster.
  • Mixing is fine, for example "A's colours with B's fonts".

The background patterns are simple geometric shapes inspired by Hausa decorative work, not copies of any specific traditional design. The Hausa words are sample wording for checking the letters and should be reviewed by a Hausa speaker before use. Dashboard numbers are examples.
