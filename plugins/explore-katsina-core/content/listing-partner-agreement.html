3. Bookings and reservations

  • You decide your prices, availability, payment options (for example full payment online, deposit online, or pay at venue) and your cancellation policy, and you must show them accurately.
  • You must honour every booking or reservation you confirm, at the price shown when the customer booked. You must keep your availability calendar correct so that customers cannot book something that is not available.
  • You must respond to booking requests promptly. Slow responses, cancellations by you and high no-show disputes may affect your ranking and, in serious cases, your listing.
  • Customers' personal data you receive through bookings may only be used to provide the booked service (see section 8).
