Getting listed

Does it cost anything to list my business?

No. A Basic listing is free. We earn a commission only on bookings made through Explore Katsina. Optional paid extras (Featured or Premium plan, Verified badge, listing setup) are on our Pricing page.

Which businesses can join?

Hotels and guest houses, restaurants, event centres and tour operators (with online booking), plus transport and car hire, crafts and souvenirs, shopping, health and emergency, and banks (directory listings).

How do I sign up?

Use List your business: a short form with your details, a map pin, photos, opening hours and your bank details for payouts. We check each listing (including your ID) before it goes live, usually within [2] working days.

Can someone set it up for me?

Yes. Our listing setup service builds your listing from your information, or with a photo visit. See Listing setup.

Bookings and money

How do I manage bookings?

From your Business Dashboard on your phone or computer: rooms, prices and calendar (hotels), menu, tables and reservations (restaurants), halls and requests (event centres). You get an email for each new booking.

When do I get paid?

Online payments come to Explore Katsina first. Your share (the payment minus commission) is released 3 days after check-out, the event or the completed reservation, if the guest has not reported a problem. You then request a payout to your bank account from the dashboard (minimum ₦5,000, placeholder).

What about "pay at the hotel" bookings?

Guests pay you directly, and the commission for those completed stays appears on a monthly invoice, which you can pay online or by transfer.

What commission do you charge?

See the current rates on our Pricing page. Each booking keeps the rate in force when it was made.

Reviews and rankings

Can I remove a bad review?

No. Reviews come only from verified guests. You can reply publicly to every review. We remove reviews only if they break our Review Guidelines (for example, abuse or personal information), never because they are negative.

Does a paid plan improve my rank?

No. Plans give you top placement marked "Featured", a highlighted map pin and more photos, but your rank number comes only from reviews, bookings and response speed.

Where are the full rules?

In the Listing & Partner Agreement, which you accept when you sign up.
