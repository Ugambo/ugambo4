Explore Katsina is a guide and booking site for Katsina State, Nigeria: hotels, restaurants, event centres, festivals, tours and things to do, in English and Hausa.

What we do

  • Book with confidence. Book hotel rooms, restaurant tables and event halls online. Payments go through Paystack or Flutterwave, and every booking has a clear cancellation policy shown before you pay.
  • Honest rankings. Places are ranked every night from verified guest reviews, completed bookings and how quickly they reply. Only guests who really stayed, ate or held an event can review. No business can pay to change its rank. Paid "Featured" places are always labelled.
  • A guide you can check. Our attraction and travel pages list their sources. Where facts are not confirmed, we say so. We never invent details about real places.
  • Fair to local businesses. Listing is free. We earn a small commission on bookings and offer optional paid promotion, all published on our Pricing page.
