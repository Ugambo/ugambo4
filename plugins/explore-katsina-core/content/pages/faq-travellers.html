Booking

How do I book a hotel, a table or an event hall?

Open the place's page, choose your dates (or date and time), and follow the steps. You will see the full price, what you pay now and the cancellation policy before you pay. You receive a confirmation email with your booking code.

Do I need an account?

No. You can book as a guest. With a free account (My Account) all your bookings, reservations, tickets and favourites are in one place. If you booked before creating an account, use "Email me the link" in My Account to add those bookings.

How do I pay?

Online with a card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack or Flutterwave. Some hotels also offer "pay at the hotel". We never see or store your card number.

Is my room or table held while I pay?

Yes, for 15 minutes. If payment is not completed in that time, the hold is released so others can book.

The hotel has not confirmed my booking yet. What happens?

Some hotels confirm each booking themselves. You will get an email when they do. If they decline, you get your money back in full.

Changes and cancellations

How do I cancel?

Open your booking (from the confirmation email or My Account) and choose Cancel. The page shows exactly how much you will get back before you confirm. See the Booking & Cancellation Policy.

Something went wrong during my stay. What can I do?

Use "Report a problem" on your booking page during your stay or up to 3 days after it. We hold the business's payment for that booking while we look into it. See the Refund Policy.

Reviews and rankings

Who can write a review?

Only guests with a completed booking made through Explore Katsina, once per booking, up to 180 days after the visit. Reviews are marked "Verified booking". See our Review Guidelines.

How are places ranked? Can businesses pay for a better rank?

Ranks are worked out every night from verified reviews (most weight), completed bookings and how quickly the business replies. Businesses cannot pay to change their rank. Paid placements are labelled "Featured" or "Promoted".

Travel

Is it safe to travel to Katsina State?

Please read the latest official travel advice before you go. We link to it on our Safety and travel tips page. We do not make our own claims about which areas are safe.

Is the site in Hausa?

Yes, use the English | Hausa switch at the top. Pages are being translated by Hausa writers. Pages not yet translated are shown in English.
