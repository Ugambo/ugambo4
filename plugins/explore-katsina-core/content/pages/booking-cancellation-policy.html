This policy explains how bookings work on Explore Katsina and what happens if you or a business cancel. The exact policy for each booking is shown on the business's page and again before you pay. That version applies to your booking.

1. Who you are booking with

Explore Katsina is a booking platform. The hotel, restaurant, event centre or tour operator provides the service and is responsible for it. Explore Katsina takes the payment on the business's behalf, keeps its commission and pays the rest to the business. Tours and event tickets sold as "Explore Katsina tours" or "tickets" are sold by Explore Katsina itself.

2. Making a booking

  • While you pay online, your room, table, hall date, seats or tickets are held for 15 minutes. If payment is not completed in time, the hold ends.
  • Hotels may confirm bookings automatically or one by one. If a hotel declines, you get a full refund.
  • Event centres offer either Request to book (the venue has 48 hours to reply; once accepted, you have 72 hours to pay the deposit) or Instant booking with a deposit.
  • Restaurants may ask for a deposit for large groups.
  • "Pay at the hotel" bookings are paid directly to the hotel.

3. Cancellation policies (hotels and event centres)

Each hotel and event centre chooses one of these. Times are counted to check-in (hotels) or to the event date (event centres).

PolicyIf you cancel
FlexibleFull refund until 24 hours before. After that, everything except the first night.
ModerateFull refund until 5 days before. After that, 50%.
Strict50% refund if you cancel at least 7 days before. Nothing after that.
Non-refundableNo refund.

4. Other bookings

  • Restaurant deposits: full refund until 24 hours before the reservation; nothing after that.
  • Food orders (where offered): full refund until the restaurant accepts the order.
  • Explore Katsina tours: full refund until 48 hours before departure.
  • Event tickets: not refundable unless the event is cancelled.

5. If the business cancels or declines

You always get back everything you paid online.

6. No-shows

If you do not arrive and did not cancel, the business may mark the booking as a no-show. Refunds then follow the booking's cancellation policy as if you cancelled on the day.

7. How to cancel

Open your booking from the confirmation email or My Account and choose Cancel. The page shows the refund amount before you confirm. Refunds are explained in our Refund Policy.
