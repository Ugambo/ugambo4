Reviews help travellers choose and help good businesses grow. To keep them trustworthy, every review on Explore Katsina is a verified review.

Who can review

  • Only guests with a booking made through Explore Katsina that has been completed: a hotel stay after check-out, a restaurant reservation after it is completed, an event after the event date, or a tour.
  • One review per booking, up to 180 days after the visit.
  • Businesses cannot review themselves or competitors, and we do not accept paid or incentivised reviews.

What to write

  • Your own, honest experience, in English or Hausa.
  • Rate the overall experience, and if you like, cleanliness, service, value and location.

What is not allowed

  • Insults, threats, hate speech or discrimination of any kind.
  • Personal information: phone numbers, addresses, or names of staff used to attack them.
  • Advertising, links or spam.
  • Content that is false, or about a different place or visit.

What we do

  • We do not edit reviews. We hide a review only if it breaks these guidelines, and every decision is logged. We never remove a review because it is negative.
  • Businesses can reply publicly to every review.
  • Rankings use verified reviews, completed bookings and response speed. Paid plans never change a rank.

Report a review

If you think a review breaks these guidelines, tell us through our contact form (choose "Report a problem or mistake").
